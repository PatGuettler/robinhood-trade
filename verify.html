Verify accuracy

Three independent ways to confirm the paper results are real and computed correctly.

1 · Automated checks

Run by the bot in GitHub Actions after every market close (and on demand). It re-walks the cash ledger, recomputes every profit and loss, checks each fill against the 5-minute bar it filled in, compares fill prices to a second independent price source, and re-runs the whole period from scratch to confirm it reproduces the same trades.

2 · Re-checked in your browser

This page recomputes the numbers from the raw CSV files with separate code (JavaScript, not the bot's Python), so a bug in the bot can't hide itself.

3 · Spot-check any trade yourself

  1. Pick a row below and click chart — it opens the stock on Yahoo Finance. Switch to a 5-minute chart for that date.
  2. Find the bar at the listed time (Eastern). Its open / high / low / close should match the Bar O/H/L/C columns.
  3. Buys fill at the bar's open plus slippage (). Limit sells fill at the target only if the bar's high reached it. Stops fill at the stop minus slippage when the low reached it.
  4. The Independent range column is that day's low–high from a different data vendor; every fill must land inside it.
